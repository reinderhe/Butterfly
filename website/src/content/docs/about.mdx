## A new branch. Shared roots.

**Longshot is a fork of [Hookshot](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot), created by NotRexed.**
Team MagicBean is continuing that work in Longshot, with its own development, fixes and community.
The original project and its contributors made this possible; their history and acknowledgements remain part of Longshot.

Longshot turns your own supported copy of the game into a native Windows or Linux program.
That process is called **static recompilation**. The build happens on your PC, and your game files stay with you.

## Made by Team MagicBean

We're here for the code, the details and the people who play. Help us make Longshot easier to use:
share a useful bug report, improve a guide, test a change or contribute a fix.
