See whether your problem has been reported, what's being worked on, and what has been released. The data comes from
[Longshot on Forgejo](https://git.magicbean.dev/MagicBeanDev/Longshot) and is refreshed every hour.

A **proposed** change is waiting for review, a **merged** one is accepted but may not be in a release yet, and a
**release** is a version you can download.

## How to read this overview

- **Reported problems** includes unconfirmed reports and questions. Open does not necessarily mean someone is working on it.
- **Feature requests** are suggestions, not promises or release dates.
- **Changes & work** includes proposed and draft changes, accepted changes, and items explicitly labelled “in progress.”
- **Releases** separates stable versions from previews. Follow the release notes for included changes.
- **Current version watch** matches exact versions in report forms. It groups reports and shows reviewed serious issues when available; it cannot diagnose a bug or measure how many players are affected.

Closed reports may be duplicates, declined requests or resolved problems. Read the linked discussion before assuming a fix.
Reviewed summaries and workarounds show their review date and sources. If the source changes, that advice is hidden until reviewed again.

The page checks its data source every five minutes while open. The “Data checked” time tells you how recent the underlying
Forgejo snapshot is. Static hosting needs a refreshed publication to update that snapshot; automatic checks alone do not make old data current.

Search and filters stay in your browser. Forgejo receives no search terms from this overview.
For instructions and troubleshooting advice, use the documentation search at the top of the site or the [common issues guide](/help/).
