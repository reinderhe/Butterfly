Butterfly Builder
=================

A window for building Butterfly (Nintendo Switch Sports Resort on PC, built on Longshot) from your own copy of the game,
and starting it. Double-click "Butterfly Builder.bat" (or the desktop shortcut it can make).

It needs these beside it:
  ButterflyBuilder.ps1        the window (Windows PowerShell, part of every Windows 10 and 11: nothing to install)
  build_ssr_longshot.bat      the build itself (scripts/build_ssr.bat in the repository), which it runs with your choices
  butterfly.ico, butterfly.png
  import_lichii_saves.bat     (optional) for "Import my Lichii save"

Your choices are kept in settings.txt beside it. The build's own log is build-longshot.log in the Butterfly folder.
